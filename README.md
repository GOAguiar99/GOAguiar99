### 🧠 About me

- 📡 I build **IoT / embedded systems** for industrial condition monitoring at TRACTIAN.
- 🚗 My master's research asks whether **crash-critical traffic** can get better service than ordinary traffic in **IEEE 802.11p vehicular Wi-Fi**, using only standard MAC mechanisms (DCF / EDCA), simulated with OMNeT++, Veins, INET and SUMO.
- ⏱️ Previously worked on **real-time scheduling** (Fixed Priority Memory-Centric over the Bao hypervisor + FreeRTOS) and **Time-Aware Shaping over Wi-Fi**.
- 🦀 Lately writing a lot of **Rust** and **Go**.
